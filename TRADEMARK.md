# NukeAll Trademark & Brand Policy

This policy governs the use of the **NukeAll** name, logos, icons, and the **`nukeall.app`** domain and brand identity ("NukeAll Marks").

While the NukeAll source code is licensed under [`GPL-3.0-or-later`](./LICENSE), **`GPL-3.0-or-later` grants rights only in copyright and applicable patents — it does NOT grant any trademark or brand license.** Trademark rights remain exclusively with the Project Maintainer and its successors and assigns.

---

## 1. What Is Permitted Without Permission

You may make truthful, non-confusing, nominative references to the NukeAll Marks, such as:

- Linking to `https://nukeall.app` or the official NukeAll GitHub repository.
- Stating accurately that a project is **"forked from NukeAll source code"** or **"based on NukeAll"**, provided your project uses a distinct name, distinct icon/logo, and distinct domain.
- Writing tutorials, security audits, academic reviews, or press articles mentioning NukeAll.
- Compiling an unmodified copy of the tagged source release strictly for your own personal, private use or security verification.

---

## 2. What Requires Explicit Written Permission

Without prior written permission from the Project Maintainer (or its successors and assigns), you may **not**:

1. **Publish modified or third-party extension packages** to the Chrome Web Store, Firefox Add-ons (AMO), Microsoft Edge Add-ons, or any other distribution channel using **"NukeAll"**, a confusingly similar name (e.g., *NukeAll Pro*, *NukeAll Plus*), or the NukeAll icons/artwork.
2. **Imply official endorsement, sponsorship, or origin** by displaying the NukeAll logo, wordmark, or store badges on unrelated products, services, websites, or forks.
3. **Register or operate look-alike domains or social handles** designed to impersonate `nukeall.app` or official NukeAll distribution channels.

---

## 3. Forking & Rebranding Guidance

If you wish to publicly distribute a modified fork under `GPL-3.0-or-later`, please:

1. Choose a new, clearly distinct extension/project name and identifier in `package.json`, `wxt.config.ts`, and `apps/website`.
2. Replace the extension icons (`apps/extension/public/icon-*.png` or equivalent assets) and website branding assets (`apps/website/public/`).
3. Retain all upstream copyright notices and the `GPL-3.0-or-later` license notice as required by the GPL.

---

## 4. Reporting Abuse & Contact

If you discover unauthorized store clones, phishing domains, or trademark misuse impersonating NukeAll, please open a GitHub Security Advisory or contact the maintainers via the official repository listed on [`nukeall.app`](https://nukeall.app).
